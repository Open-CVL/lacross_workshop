01 / The workshop

Cross the gap between ground and sky.

The same place can look radically different from the pavement, a drone, a satellite, or a moving robot. LaCross brings together the communities working to connect these complementary perspectives—turning visual difference into a shared understanding of the world.

We welcome research spanning geometry, learning, generation, and real-world deployment. Alongside invited talks and a paper track, LaCross will host a cross-view localization competition built around the OpenCVL benchmark.

Cross-view scene understanding
Novel-view synthesis & 3D modelling
Automated mapping & self-driving
World models & VLAs
Retrieval & localization
Robot collaboration
Societal benefits of cross-view vision
02 / Cross-view localization challenge

Put your method on the map.
